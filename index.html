🐟 Stockfish — After-game review

Play your own game first. When you finish, Stockfish can help you revisit up to three important moments. It never suggests moves while Bella is playing.

Stockfish is optional. To use it, put the engine's JS and WASM files in the engine/ folder on GitHub.

Engine: Stockfish 19 Lite single-threaded, open-source GPLv3. Its quick evaluations are approximate, especially in complicated positions. The review is for learning, not grading.

🌟 My learning progress

Learning chess is not just about winning. We are building the habit of looking, thinking and checking before moving.

For parents: Do not rush to give the answer. Ask Bella: “What is the opponent attacking?” “Is that square safe?” “What happens if the opponent captures?” One short lesson, three times a week, is enough to build a routine.
About these lessons
This is an independent, original interactive practice course organised around ideas covered in Learning Chess — Workbook Step 1 by Rob Brunia and Cor van Wijgerden: attacking, defending, unprotected pieces and favourable exchanges. The practice boards and explanations were created for this site; textbook exercises are not reproduced.
Made for curious young chess thinkers · English-only interface · Lessons work without an account. Stockfish review needs the local engine assets and a web server. Your lesson progress and coach practice totals stay in this browser's local storage. No data is uploaded or shared.
